FREQUENTLY ASKED

Common questions.

How do I request a meet and greet? +

Visit our Packages page, select the experience you'd like and complete the booking request form. Our team will review the request and contact you regarding availability.

Does submitting a request guarantee a meeting? +

No. A booking request is not a confirmed appointment. Availability must be reviewed and confirmed by the experience team.

Can I request a private event? +

Yes. Select "Private Event" when contacting the team and include details about your event, preferred date and location.

Can I request a specific date? +

Yes. You can provide your preferred date when submitting a booking request. Dates remain subject to availability.

How will I know if my request is confirmed? +

The experience team will contact you directly using the information provided in your request.
