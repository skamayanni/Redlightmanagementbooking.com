DONATION FAQ

Questions about giving?

Can I choose how much I donate? +

Yes. You can select one of the suggested amounts or enter a custom contribution.

Can I donate using cryptocurrency? +

Cryptocurrency contributions can be accepted when the campaign has a verified wallet and appropriate payment arrangements in place.

Are donations refundable? +

Refund policies depend on the organization and payment provider receiving the contribution. Please contact the organization before donating if you need clarification.

How do I know the payment details are genuine? +

Always verify payment information through the organization's official communication channels before transferring funds.
